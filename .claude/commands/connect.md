Find meaningful connections in my vault.

Focus: $ARGUMENTS

## Before you start

1. Read CLAUDE.md first and follow it throughout.
2. If a focus is given above, investigate that topic.
3. If no focus is given, examine recently created or modified notes.
4. Check existing Maps of Content in _system/maps.
5. Search the vault for meaningful relationships. Read relevant notes fully.
6. Do not modify, create, move or delete any notes at this stage.

## Look specifically for

- The same idea appearing in different contexts
- Notes that reinforce each other
- Contradictions
- A note that answers a question raised elsewhere
- Principles and examples
- Company and industry relationships
- Investment theses and supporting evidence
- Ideas that should be combined
- Important isolated notes

## For every proposed connection

- Identify the relevant notes using [[wikilinks]].
- Explain WHY they are connected.
- Distinguish evidence from inference. Never present an inference as a fact.
- Only propose genuine relationships. Do not link notes for the sake of it.

## Also identify

- Contradictions
- Clusters of 5+ related notes
- Important isolated notes
- Unanswered questions

If a cluster of 5+ genuinely related notes exists, propose a Map of Content under _system/maps/.

## Report

Present findings in this order:

1. Proposed connections
2. Contradictions
3. Clusters (and any proposed Map of Content)
4. Important isolated notes
5. Unanswered questions

If there are no meaningful connections, say so. Do not invent them.

Then stop and wait for my approval. Only after I approve, create or modify the approved links or Maps of Content, and nothing else. Never silently merge potentially different concepts. If approved changes would modify more than 20 files, confirm again before proceeding.
