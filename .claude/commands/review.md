Review recent activity in my vault.

Period: $ARGUMENTS (if blank, use the last 7 days)

## Before you start

1. Read CLAUDE.md first and follow it throughout.
2. Find notes created or modified in the period. Use file dates and any dates in frontmatter or note text.
3. Review especially:
   - notes created or modified recently
   - recent daily notes in 50_daily
   - recent projects in 10_projects
   - recent resources in 30_resources
   - unresolved questions (QUESTION labels and Open questions sections)
   - recent connections (new links and Maps of Content in _system/maps)
   - isolated notes (few or no links to or from them)
4. Read the relevant notes fully.
5. Use only information in the vault. Do not supplement it with outside knowledge or invent information.
6. Do not modify, create, move or delete any notes or MOCs.

## Review

Write a concise weekly-style review with these sections:

## What I learned
Important new ideas or knowledge captured.

## What I am working on
Active projects and their current state.

## Emerging themes
Patterns appearing across multiple notes.

## Important connections
Recently discovered relationships that matter.

## Open questions
Questions that remain unanswered in the vault.

## Decisions / actions
Things that appear to require my attention.

## Neglected areas
Important projects or notes that appear inactive.

## Rules

- Prioritise what needs my attention over describing everything in the vault.
- Keep each section short.
- Label claims clearly as MY VIEW, SOURCE CLAIM, FACT, INFERENCE or QUESTION.
- Never present an inference as a fact. Judgements such as "appears neglected" or "needs attention" are INFERENCE.
- Cite relevant notes using [[wikilinks]].
- If there is not enough information for a section, say so.
