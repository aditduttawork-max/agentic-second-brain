# CLAUDE.md

## Who I am

I am Aditya.

I work in investment banking / investor relations and spend
significant time researching companies, industries, markets,
investing, AI and business.

My second brain should help me:
- remember what I learn
- connect ideas
- develop investment theses
- improve decisions
- turn ideas into useful outputs

I capture.
You organize, connect, resurface and use.

## What this vault is

This is my personal knowledge system.

Do not turn my notes into generic AI-written content.
Preserve my thinking.

## Folder structure

00_inbox: Raw captures. Everything new goes here.

10_projects: Things with a defined finish line.

20_areas: Ongoing responsibilities and interests.

30_resources: Knowledge and topics I am learning about.

40_archive: Finished, abandoned or obsolete material.

50_daily: Daily notes.

60_outputs: Content, decisions and reviews generated from
my knowledge.

_system: Templates, maps and operating instructions.

## Routing rules

When processing 00_inbox:

- Projects: work with a defined outcome or finish line.
- Areas: ongoing responsibilities, interests or domains.
- Resources: knowledge, research, concepts and reference material.
- Archive: obsolete, completed or abandoned material.
- If classification is uncertain, keep the note in 00_inbox and ask.
- Do not create a new project, area or resource category unless there is a clear reason.

## Daily notes

Daily notes use the format YYYY_MM_DD.

Daily notes are stored in 50_daily/.

## Note rules

1. Search the vault before creating a note.
2. Prefer updating an existing note when appropriate.
3. One meaningful idea per note where practical.
4. Use lowercase_with_underscores filenames.
5. Every substantive note starts with a one-line summary.
6. Preserve my original thinking.
7. Clean grammar and structure without changing my opinions.
8. Use [[wikilinks]] for meaningful relationships.
9. Preserve source information.
10. Add a Links section to substantive notes.

## Evidence

Distinguish between:

FACT
SOURCE CLAIM
MANAGEMENT GUIDANCE
MY VIEW
INFERENCE
QUESTION

Never turn an inference into a fact.

## Safety

Never delete notes.

Move obsolete material to 40_archive.

Never silently merge potentially different concepts.

Never make large structural changes without asking.

Never modify large numbers of files without asking.

- Before modifying more than 20 files, ask for approval.

## Questions

Use the vault first.

If the notes do not contain the answer, say:

"Your notes don't cover this."

Do not silently supplement the answer with outside knowledge.

## Style

Answer first.

Be concise.

Use evidence.

- Inside notes: cite supporting notes with [[wikilinks]].
- In queue result files: do not use wikilinks. Follow the source rules in .claude/commands/run.md.
